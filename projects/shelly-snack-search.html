About Shelly's Snack Search

Shelly's Snack Search is an arcade platformer game where you play as Shelly the spider on the hunt for her lunch. Build webs to traverse the lost & found box and capture as many bugs as possible!

This game was created in 48 hours for McGameJam 2025. This was my first time as a team lead for a game jam, and I definitely learned a lot. My focus was to create a relatively simple yet enjoyable game that our team could feasibly create under the time constraints. I designed the level and the mechanics, as well as directed and organized the team of artists and programmers.

Responsibilities

What I Did

  • 01 Designed the gameplay loop and the core web-shooting mechanic.
  • 02 Outlined the level layout and necessary assets and then built the level in Unity.
  • 03 Performed UI and UX polish by delving into code and making fixes
  • 04 Implemented the audio system and SFX to provide useful feedback to players
  • 05 Coordinated a team working together for the first time through the entire development cycle under time constraints
